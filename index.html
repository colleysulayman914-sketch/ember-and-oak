About us

Ember & Oak started as a weekend market stall run by two friends with one cast-iron pan. Today we are a 40-seat café on Harbour Street, but the idea is the same: simple food, cooked well, with ingredients from farms within 50 km.

Our space is family friendly, wheelchair accessible and dog friendly on the terrace.

  • Local first

    Eggs, dairy and vegetables from nearby farms.

  • Baked daily

    Sourdough and pastries out of the oven by 7 a.m.

  • Low waste

    Compostable takeaway cups and a refill discount.
